    \begin{tablenotes}\footnotesize
    \item \textit{Notes:} Error rates are the share of generation attempts
    that produced non-transient failures (schema-validation failures and
    unparseable output; transient rate-limit and network errors are
    excluded). Rates are computed as errors divided by total attempts
    (valid responses plus errors) per model, pooled across all conditions
    within each study. These figures are approximate: run restarts,
    graph-structure changes, and cleanup cycles introduce attribution
    noise, and a small number of errors could not be linked to a specific
    model. All persistent failures (after four total attempts) are coded as
    missing and excluded from the analytic sample, so these error rates do
    not affect the validity of reported estimates; they indicate only which
    models required more regeneration to reach the target sample. GPT-5 is
    omitted from this table because it was run only once (see
    Table~\ref{tab:models}). Llama-4 Scout's substantially elevated Study~1
    error rate is consistent with its degenerate bid-response behavior
    discussed in Section~\ref{sec:results}.
